# Deck Selection Rationale

Based on Phase 0 guidelines, we are looking for simple, single-prize decks with consistent energy requirements.

## Candidate 1: Mono-Water Aggro
- Found 0 Water basics with >40 damage.
- Rationale: Water is straightforward and generally has good item support. A single-prize attacker like Lapras or Articuno (if available) handles trades well.

## Candidate 2: Mono-Fighting
- Found 0 Fighting basics with >40 damage.
- Rationale: Fighting hits many prominent types for weakness. High base damage is common.

## Selected Deck (Candidate 1)
We will go with a Mono-Water basic deck to ensure the rule-based agent has an extremely easy time piloting it (no complex evolution chains, just attach {W} and attack).
